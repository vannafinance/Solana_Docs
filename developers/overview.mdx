## Current scope

Seven reserves (SOL, USDC, TSLAx, GOOGLx, AAPLx, ANTHROPIC, OPENAI). Isolated margin. Jupiter margin swaps. Long/short recipes. Lite/Kamino. Fork faucet. Pyth (or fork price refresh).

There is no published `@vanna/sdk` package. Copy the PDA helpers and instruction builders from `Backend-Solana/lib/solana`.
