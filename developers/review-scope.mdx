These pages describe the current `Protocol_V1_Solana` and `Backend-Solana` trees used for the Stocklana demo.

## Claimed

- Instruction surface in `lib.rs`
- PDA seeds and account layouts in `state/`
- Math in `math/`
- App routes and config addresses in `lib/solana/config.ts`
- Hosted demo URLs as operated for the hackathon

## Not claimed

- A completed public-mainnet launch
- An external audit opinion (internal reports exist in the protocol repo; they are not a substitute)
- That Kamino/Jupiter/Pyth remain available or identically configured tomorrow
- That the `devnet` hostname is Solana public Devnet
- Continuous sync of the fork with mainnet after clone time
- A published npm SDK

If a filename says `SECURITY_AUDIT_REPORT`, read the file. Do not infer a firm, a date of sign-off, or a bounty from the name alone.
