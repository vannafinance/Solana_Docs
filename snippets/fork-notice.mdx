<Note>
Vanna runs on a **Solana mainnet fork** (Surfpool), not public Devnet or Mainnet. Balances are test balances on the fork. Add the fork RPC `https://rpc-devnet.solana.vanna.finance` to your wallet ([how](/guides/connect-wallet#add-the-vanna-rpc-to-your-wallet)), then use **Faucet** before any Earn, Margin, Trade, Farm or Predict action.
</Note>
