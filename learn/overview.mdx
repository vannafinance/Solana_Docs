Vanna on Solana is a lending and margin protocol. Lenders supply USDC, USDT or SOL to pools. Traders borrow from those pools through one cross-margin account per wallet, and put the borrowed funds to work in whitelisted protocols: Jupiter and Titan swaps, Kamino lending, Phoenix perps, Perena's USD* vaults, and Vanna's delta-neutral basis vaults. Every position counts toward one health factor.

The protocol is three programs: a **core** that only does accounting, an **oracle** that values every holding, and a **validator** that decides which external calls a margin account may make.
