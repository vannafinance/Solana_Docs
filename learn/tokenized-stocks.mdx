## What already existed

| Token | Issuer / venue | Program | Decimals |
|---|---|---|---|
| TSLAx | xStocks | Token-2022 | 8 |
| GOOGLx | xStocks | Token-2022 | 8 |
| AAPLx | xStocks | Token-2022 | 8 |
| ANTHROPIC | [PreStocks](https://prestocks.com) | Token-2022 | 9 |
| OPENAI | [PreStocks](https://prestocks.com) | Token-2022 | 9 |

They trade on DEXes. Pyth publishes equity and xStock feeds (`Equity.US.AAPL/USD`, `Crypto.AAPLX/USD`, and siblings). Kamino listed several xStocks.

## What was missing

1. **No interest.** Kamino xStocks utilization is ~0, so supply APY is ~0. Holding in Kamino was still just holding.
2. **No one-click credit.** No path from "I own TSLAx" to "that TSLAx is working in a market that pays."

## What Vanna added

- A separate **lending pool** for each token — borrowers create a rate
- **Margin credit** against the same tokens
- **Long / short** via borrow + Jupiter (real tokens, not a virtual future)
- **Farm** that routes stock equity into Kamino **main-market** USDC/SOL, where APY is real

PreStocks (ANTHROPIC, OPENAI) carry a genuine Token-2022 `TransferFeeConfig` extension at 100bps (1%) per transfer — a real third-party fee Vanna did not create, just accounts for. xStocks (TSLAx, GOOGLx, AAPLx) have no transfer fee. Either way, fees are measured on every vault move, not assumed: quotes and health use the amount that actually arrived, not the amount requested.

This is the Stocklana wedge: not another stock wrapper — **utilities around the wrappers that already shipped**.
