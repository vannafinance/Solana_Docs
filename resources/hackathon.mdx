Vanna Solana was built for **[Stocklana](https://hackathons.solana.com/hackathons/stocklana)** — tokenized stocks on Solana, judged on whether this can be a real app people use.

## The product question we answered

Tokenized stocks already trade. They did not **do** anything.

1. **No perp market** → we shipped long/short for TSLAx, GOOGLx, AAPLx, OPENAI, ANTHROPIC.
2. **Kamino stock interest is zero** → we shipped Vanna lending pools where borrow demand creates APY.
3. **Hold had no utility** → we shipped one-click Farm: stock in, Kamino main-market USDC/SOL yield out.

- Jupiter routes inside the margin vault
- Kamino CPI + main-market supply ([credit / yield](https://hackathons.solana.com/hackathons/stocklana) wedge)

## Judge path (five minutes)

1. Open the app. Connect. Faucet → Mint All.
2. Supply 10 TSLAx on Earn. See a non-zero APY.
3. Perps → Long TSLAx with 10 USDC at 2×. One signature. Row appears.
4. Perps → Short a PreStock (ANTHROPIC or OPENAI). That market did not exist.
5. Farm → 1 TSLAx at 2× into Kamino USDC. Open Position. Exit 100%.
