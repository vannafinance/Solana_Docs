A long says: I want more stock than my USDC can buy at 1×.

## Example

You deposit **10 USDC** at 2×.

1. Margin receives 10 USDC.
2. Vanna borrows another 10 USDC against it.
3. Jupiter swaps **20 USDC** into TSLAx.
4. Vault holds ~0.05 TSLAx (at ~$378). Debt is 10 USDC plus interest.

If TSLAx rises, collateral USD rises and health improves. If it falls, health falls. Liquidation price is shown on the row.

## Steps

1. Open **Perps** and pick the pair.
2. Select **Long**.
3. Enter USDC collateral and leverage.
4. Read position size, health, and liquidation price.
5. Confirm one signature.
6. Find the row under **Current Positions**. Close from that row.

You pay **USDC borrow APR**, not a funding rate. Time is not free.
