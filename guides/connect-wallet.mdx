## Supported wallets

Vanna uses the Solana **Wallet Standard**, so any wallet that registers itself is detected automatically. Tested with:

- [Phantom](https://phantom.com/download)
- [Solflare](https://solflare.com/download)
- [Backpack](https://backpack.app/download)

Use a **test** wallet. Nothing on the fork is real money, but there is no reason to expose a wallet that holds real funds.

## Add the Vanna RPC to your wallet

Vanna runs on a Solana **mainnet fork**. The app already reads and sends every transaction through the fork RPC, but your wallet also needs it: otherwise the wallet shows your real Mainnet balances and simulates transactions against Mainnet, so previews look wrong or fail.

```text
https://rpc-devnet.solana.vanna.finance
```

The purple strip at the top of every page shows this URL with a **Copy** button.

## Connect

1. Open [test.solana.vanna.finance](https://test.solana.vanna.finance).
2. Click the wallet button at the top right and pick your wallet.
3. Approve the connection. Your shortened address appears in the header.
4. Wait a moment for balances and any existing margin account to load from the fork.

The first time you open the site you may be asked for an **access code**. Enter it once; your browser remembers it for 30 days.

## Same wallet, same account

Your margin account is derived from your wallet address. Reconnect with the same wallet to return to the same collateral, debt, Farm and Predict positions. A different address has a different account.
