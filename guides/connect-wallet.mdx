## Supported wallet

The app connects with **[Backpack](https://backpack.app/download)**, a Solana Wallet Standard wallet.

## Connect

1. Open [devnet.solana.vanna.finance](https://devnet.solana.vanna.finance/portfolio) and choose **Connect Wallet**.
2. Pick **Backpack** and approve the connection in the extension.
3. Confirm the displayed address.
4. Wait for balances and any existing margin account to load from the fork.

The site sends every transaction to the hosted fork (`https://rpc-devnet.solana.vanna.finance`) itself; Backpack only signs. Setting Backpack's RPC to the same fork (below) makes Backpack show your **fork** balances and preview transactions against the fork instead of public Mainnet.

## Set a custom RPC in Backpack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Backpack">
    Open the Backpack extension. With its default RPC it shows your public Mainnet balances, not the fork.

    <Frame caption="Backpack on its default RPC. Click the image to zoom.">
      <img src="/images/wallet/1-open-wallet.png" alt="Backpack wallet home screen showing a 0 SOL balance on the default RPC" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the **account icon** (top left), then **Settings**.

    <Frame caption="Account menu with Settings. Click the image to zoom.">
      <img src="/images/wallet/2-open-settings.png" alt="Backpack account menu open with the Settings option highlighted" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Solana">
    Click **Solana** (not Solana Devnet).

    <Frame caption="Settings: pick the Solana network. Click the image to zoom.">
      <img src="/images/wallet/3-select-solana.png" alt="Backpack settings list with Solana and Solana Devnet entries" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open RPC Connection">
    Click **RPC Connection**. It shows **Backpack Default RPC** until you change it.

    <Frame caption="Solana settings: RPC Connection. Click the image to zoom.">
      <img src="/images/wallet/4-rpc-connection.png" alt="Backpack Solana settings showing RPC Connection set to Backpack Default RPC" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Custom">
    Click **Custom**.

    <Frame caption="RPC Connection: choose Custom. Click the image to zoom.">
      <img src="/images/wallet/5-custom.png" alt="Backpack RPC Connection screen with Backpack Default RPC and Custom options" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the Vanna RPC and click Update">
    Paste `https://rpc-devnet.solana.vanna.finance` into the **RPC** field and click **Update**.

    <Frame caption="Change RPC Connection with the Vanna fork URL. Click the image to zoom.">
      <img src="/images/wallet/6-paste-rpc.png" alt="Backpack Change RPC Connection screen with the Vanna fork RPC URL entered and an Update button" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Backpack now shows **Custom Rpc: https://rpc-devnet.solana.vanna.finance** at the top of the wallet panel, and your balances match the app. Reconnect on [devnet.solana.vanna.finance](https://devnet.solana.vanna.finance/portfolio) if the site was already open.

<Warning>
If you run a **local** Surfpool node instead (see [Run and Setup](/guides/setup)), use `http://127.0.0.1:8899` as the custom RPC instead of the hosted URL.
</Warning>

Use the same Backpack account to return to the same margin account. A different address has different holdings, debt, and Farm positions.

Continue with [Earn](/guides/earn/overview) or [Open a Margin Account](/guides/margin/open-account).
