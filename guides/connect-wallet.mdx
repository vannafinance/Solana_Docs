## Supported wallets

Vanna uses the Solana **Wallet Standard**, so any wallet that registers itself is detected automatically. Tested with:

- [Phantom](https://phantom.com/download)
- [Solflare](https://solflare.com/download)
- [Backpack](https://backpack.app/download)

Use a **test** wallet. Nothing on the fork is real money, but there is no reason to expose a wallet that holds real funds.

## Add the Vanna RPC to your wallet

Vanna runs on a Solana **mainnet fork**. The app already reads and sends every transaction through the fork RPC, but your wallet also needs it: otherwise the wallet shows your real Mainnet balances and simulates transactions against Mainnet, so previews look wrong or fail.

```text
https://rpc-devnet.solana.vanna.finance
```

The purple strip at the top of every page shows this URL with a **Copy** button.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Phantom">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open settings">Open Phantom, click your **account icon** (top left), then **Settings**.</Step>
      <Step title="Developer settings">Open **Developer Settings** and turn on **Testnet Mode** if Phantom asks for it before showing network options.</Step>
      <Step title="Custom RPC">Under **Solana**, choose the custom RPC option and paste `https://rpc-devnet.solana.vanna.finance`. Save.</Step>
    </Steps>
    Menu names move between Phantom releases. If you can't find a custom RPC field, use Solflare or Backpack, which expose it directly.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Solflare">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open settings">Open Solflare and go to **Settings → Network**.</Step>
      <Step title="Add a custom node">Choose **Custom node** (or **Add custom RPC**) and paste `https://rpc-devnet.solana.vanna.finance`.</Step>
      <Step title="Select it">Save and make sure the custom node is the selected network.</Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Backpack">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open settings">Click the **account icon** (top left), then **Settings**.</Step>
      <Step title="Select Solana">Click **Solana** (not Solana Devnet).</Step>
      <Step title="RPC Connection">Click **RPC Connection**, then **Custom**.</Step>
      <Step title="Paste and update">Paste `https://rpc-devnet.solana.vanna.finance` into the RPC field and click **Update**.</Step>
    </Steps>
    Backpack then shows **Custom Rpc** at the top of the wallet panel.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
Running the stack on your own machine? Use your local node `http://127.0.0.1:8899` instead (see [Run It Yourself](/guides/setup)).

## Connect

1. Open [test.solana.vanna.finance](https://test.solana.vanna.finance).
2. Click the wallet button at the top right and pick your wallet.
3. Approve the connection. Your shortened address appears in the header.
4. Wait a moment for balances and any existing margin account to load from the fork.

The first time you open the site you may be asked for an **access code**. Enter it once; your browser remembers it for 30 days.

## Same wallet, same account

Your margin account is derived from your wallet address. Reconnect with the same wallet to return to the same collateral, debt, Farm and Predict positions. A different address has a different account.
