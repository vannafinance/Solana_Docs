Tokenized stocks already trade on Solana. TSLAx, GOOGLx, AAPLx, OPENAI, and ANTHROPIC exist. The market still treated them like pictures of equities: buy, sit, hope the price goes up.

Vanna, built for [Stocklana](https://hackathons.solana.com/hackathons/stocklana), gives listed and pre-IPO stock tokens three uses beyond holding.

## Three things Vanna adds

### 1. Leveraged longs and shorts on stock tokens

Vanna lists a perps market for each supported stock token, listed (xStocks) and pre-IPO (PreStocks), at up to 5×. Positions open from the same cross-margin account as Farm and Swap, and are built from borrowed tokens plus a Jupiter swap, so there is no funding rate.

**Vanna Perps** opens and closes long/short stock exposure from a margin account. Long deposits USDC, borrows more USDC, and swaps the total into the stock. Short deposits the stock, borrows more of that stock, and swaps the total into USDC. Both paths are one signed Solana transaction.

[Trade TSLAx / USDC →](/guides/perps/overview)

### 2. Kamino stock interest was zero

Kamino listed xStocks. Utilization on those stock reserves is effectively unused, so **supply APY sits at 0%**. Holding TSLAx in Kamino did not pay you.

Vanna stands up **Credit LP pools** for the same stocks. Borrowers pay a kink-model APR. Credit LPs receive vTokens that grow with that interest. Short sellers borrow from these pools, so supplying TSLAx, GOOGLx, AAPLx, OPENAI, and ANTHROPIC earns interest.

[Supply TSLAx →](/guides/earn/overview)

### 3. One-click yield on stock collateral

**Farm / One-Click** deposits a stock or PreStock, borrows USDC or SOL against it from Vanna, and supplies that into **Kamino's main market**. The stock stays as collateral. Kamino's main market is the reserve that has real utilization and a real supply APY. Close is the reverse unwind.

[Open a carry trade →](/guides/farm/one-click)

## Before and after

| What you can do | On Solana today | With Vanna |
|---|---|---|
| Hold TSLAx / OPENAI | Yes | Yes |
| Earn interest on the stock itself | ~0% on Kamino's xStocks market | Yes — Vanna Credit LP pools, paid by short sellers |
| Borrow against the stock | Cash loans on Kamino | Cross-margin account, deployable into Perps, Farm and Swap |
| Long / short the stock token with margin | — | Up to 5×, Jupiter-routed, no funding rate |
| One-click leveraged yield | — | Stock collateral → borrow USDC/SOL → Kamino main market |

## What this is not

Vanna does not issue the stocks. xStocks and PreStocks already exist. We do not replace Kamino. Vanna complements Kamino: it lends USDC or SOL against stock collateral into Kamino's main market, and adds a credit and perps layer on top of the stock tokens.
