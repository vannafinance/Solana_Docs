## What you can do

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Borrow without selling | Deposit SOL, LSTs, stables or xStocks and borrow SOL, USDC or USDT ([Borrow](/guides/margin/borrow)) |
| Leverage up to 10× | Use the leverage slider on Margin; borrow one asset or two at once with Dual Borrow |
| Swap with leverage | Swap inside the margin account on Jupiter or Titan ([Spot](/guides/trade/spot-swap)) |
| Long or short | Phoenix perps on SOL, ETH, BTC, TSLA and META, up to 5× per order ([Perps](/guides/perps/overview)) |
| Leveraged yield | Supply to Kamino, or deposit into Perena or delta-neutral basis vaults ([Farm](/guides/farm/overview)) |
| Close everything | Flash repay closes positions and repays every loan in one guided flow ([Repay](/guides/margin/repay)) |

## Your numbers

- **Health factor**: collateral ÷ debt. Liquidation at 1.10 or below. See [Health Factor](/guides/margin/health-factor).
- **Leverage**: how large your exposure is relative to your own equity.
- **Collateral left before liquidation**: how much value your collateral can lose before the account reaches 1.10.

Every page that can change your account shows a before → after preview of these numbers before you sign.
