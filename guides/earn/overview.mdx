**Earn** lists Vanna's lending pools. Supplying to a pool lends your tokens to margin accounts; their interest is what you earn.

## Pools

| Pool | Receipt | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SOL | vSOL | Borrowed for SOL longs and SOL-denominated strategies |
| USDC | vUSDC | Usually the busiest pool: most leverage, perps collateral and vaults run on USDC |
| USDT | vUSDT | Stablecoin pool |

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Assets Supplied** | Total supplied to the pool, in tokens and USD |
| **Supply APY** | What lenders currently earn |
| **Assets Borrowed** | What margin accounts currently owe the pool |
| **Borrow APY** | What borrowers currently pay |
| **Utilization Rate** | Borrowed ÷ supplied. Rates rise with utilization |
| **Your Supply** | Your share of the pool, in tokens and USD |

Use **Vaults / Positions** to switch between all pools and just yours, and the filters to search or sort. The charts above the table show the pool's supply and rate history.

## Pool page

Click a pool for its detail page: Total Supply, Available Liquidity, Utilization and Supply APY on top, then tabs for **Your Positions**, **Details** (statistics, oracle price, liquidation bonus), **Activity**, **Collateral and Limits** and **Analytics**. The supply / withdraw panel is on the right.

## Your receipt: vTokens

Supplying mints vTokens to your wallet. The number of vTokens you hold stays the same; what each one is worth in the underlying asset (the exchange rate, e.g. "SOL per vSOL") rises as interest accrues. See [vTokens](/learn/vtokens).
