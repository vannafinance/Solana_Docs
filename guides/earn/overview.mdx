Earn is wallet-to-pool supply. Deposits receive vTokens — a claim on the pool's cash plus borrower interest.

This is the utility Kamino's xStocks market does not offer. Those stock reserves sit at ~0% APY. Vanna pools for the same mints pay a live borrow rate.

Each mint has its own reserve, share mint, and rate curve. Supplying TSLAx does not earn the USDC pool's rate.

## Use Earn

1. Open **Earn**.
2. Pick a pool.
3. Use **Supply Liquidity** or **Withdraw Liquidity**.
4. Confirm the wallet signature and wait for balances to refresh.

Supply uses wallet funds. No margin account is required. Earnings depend on actual borrower interest and utilization — they are not a fixed coupon.

See [Supply](/guides/earn/supply) and [Withdraw](/guides/earn/withdraw).
