---
title: "Withdraw"
description: "Redeem vTokens for the underlying asset plus the interest it earned."
mode: "wide"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pool">
    On [Earn](/guides/earn/overview), open the pool you supplied to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Withdraw Liquidity">
    Switch the right-hand panel to **Withdraw Liquidity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Enter how much of the underlying asset to withdraw, or use the % chips. **100%** redeems all your vTokens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Check the preview, click withdraw and approve in your wallet. The vTokens are burned and the underlying (with its accrued interest) arrives in your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the pool is busy

You can only withdraw cash the pool is holding right now, shown as **Available Liquidity** (supplied minus borrowed). In a pool with very high utilization, a large withdrawal may have to wait until borrowers repay or are liquidated. High utilization also pushes rates up, which encourages repayment and new supply.

## Interest

Interest accrues continuously. The amount you receive is your vTokens × the current exchange rate, so it already includes everything earned since you supplied.
