## Repay a loan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Repay Loan">
    On **Margin**, open the **Repay Loan** tab, or click **Repay** next to a debt in the Positions table or on Portfolio (it opens this tab with that asset selected).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the asset">
    The tab shows your exact on-chain debt for that asset, interest included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the source">
    **MB** repays from the tokens already in your margin account. **WB** repays from your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Type an amount or use the % chips. **100%** is a full repay: it closes the debt position entirely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Check the before → after preview and click **Pay Now**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**A full repay from MB is often a little short.** Interest keeps accruing, so the borrowed tokens in your margin account are usually slightly less than the debt. A full repay needs the debt plus a small (0.2%) buffer for interest that accrues while the transaction lands. If the margin account doesn't have it, the tab tells you and suggests repaying from your wallet (WB) instead.
</Note>

## Flash repay

**Flash repay** (on the Margin positions header and in the Repay tab) unwinds the whole account for you, in steps:

| Step | What it does |
|---|---|
| 1. Perps | Closes every Phoenix position (reduce-only market orders) and brings the USDC back to the margin account |
| 2. Farm | Redeems every Kamino receipt back to its underlying token |
| 3. Swap | For each loan the account can't cover in that same token, swaps other holdings into it (stables first, then the largest holding) through the best route |
| 4. Repay | Repays every loan in full and closes its debt position |
| 5. Transfer (optional) | **Flash transfer** also withdraws everything left to your wallet |

Each step is its own transaction and wallet approval, with progress shown as it goes. You don't have to watch it: close the window (×, Escape or a click outside) and it keeps running as a small card at the bottom right with the current step and a progress bar. **View** opens the full window again; once it finishes, the card shows Done or the error and can be closed. It is **not** one atomic transaction. Every step re-reads the chain first, so if a run stops half-way you simply run it again and it continues with whatever is left. It never repays more than you owe; if the account can't cover its debt, the swap step stops and shows the shortfall.

## After repaying

When all debt is repaid the health factor reads **No debt**. Your collateral stays in the margin account; withdraw it with [Transfer Collateral → WB](/guides/margin/transfer-collateral).
