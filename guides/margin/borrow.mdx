Borrowing happens in the **Leverage your Assets** tab, together with (or without) a deposit.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the deposit">
    Choose collateral under **Deposit** (or leave it empty to borrow against what's already in the account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Single or Dual Borrow">
    **Single Borrow** borrows one asset. **Dual Borrow** splits the loan across two assets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the asset">
    Choose SOL, USDC or USDT under **Borrow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the size">
    Drag the leverage slider (**1× to 10×**), use **−/+**, type an amount, or click **Max Value**. **Total** and **Max** show the loan in USD.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and sign">
    Check **Transaction Details**: the account's health factor, leverage and collateral before → after. Click **Deposit & Borrow** and approve.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where the loan goes

Borrowed tokens land in your **margin account**, not your wallet. From there you can swap, trade perps or farm. To take them to your wallet, use [Transfer Collateral → WB](/guides/margin/transfer-collateral); that withdrawal is health-checked.

## Limits

- The program rejects any borrow that would leave the account at a health factor of **1.10 or below**.
- The app's Max keeps a **1.15** safety floor so a small price move right after borrowing doesn't put you at the line.
- You can't borrow more than the pool has available (see the pool's Available Liquidity on [Earn](/guides/earn/overview)).

## Interest

Debt grows at the pool's **Borrow APY**, which rises with the pool's utilization. **Net Borrow Rate** on the Margin page is your account's blended rate; accrued interest shows in the Positions table and on Portfolio.
