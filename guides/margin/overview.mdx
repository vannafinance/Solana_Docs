The **Margin** page is where you manage your margin account: deposit collateral, borrow, repay and move collateral in or out.

## Account stats

| Stat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Net Health Factor** | Collateral ÷ debt. Liquidatable at 1.10 or below. **No debt** when you owe nothing |
| **Collateral Left Before Liquidation** | How much value your collateral can lose before the account hits 1.10 |
| **Net Available Collateral** | Collateral minus debt: your equity |
| **Net amount Borrowed** | Total debt in USD, including accrued interest |
| **Net Leverage Taken** | Everything the account holds (borrowed funds included, open perps at full size) ÷ your equity. 1.00x = no borrowing |

## The three tabs

| Tab | What it does | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Leverage your Assets** | Deposit collateral and borrow in one transaction, with a 1×–10× leverage slider | [Deposit](/guides/margin/deposit-collateral) · [Borrow](/guides/margin/borrow) |
| **Repay Loan** | Repay from your margin account or your wallet; Flash repay closes everything | [Repay](/guides/margin/repay) |
| **Transfer Collateral** | Move tokens between your wallet and your margin account | [Transfer Collateral](/guides/margin/transfer-collateral) |

## Margin Account Info

The right column shows Total Borrowed value, Net Available Collateral, Total Value, Net Health Factor and Net Borrow Rate, then:

- **Liquidation**: your health factor on a bar, the 1.10 liquidation line and the dollar buffer left before it.
- **Oracles and LTs**: the oracle program, the 1.10× liquidation threshold, the Vanna program address, and every token's live price with its source (Kamino Scope or Pyth) and freshness.

## Positions

<Frame caption="Positions: everything the account holds, what it owes, leverage, health and interest. Click the image to zoom.">
  <img src="/images/Margin3.png" alt="Positions table showing collateral deposited including SOL, kSOL, USDC, USD* vault receipts and kUSDC, borrowed USDC, leverage, health factor and a Repay button" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</Frame>

**Current Positions** shows one row for the whole account: every collateral (including Farm receipts such as kSOL, kUSDC, USD\* and vbSOL), every borrowed asset with its share of debt, leverage, health factor, interest accrued and a **Repay** button. **Positions History** lists past actions. The **Spot** and **Farm** chips jump to those positions, and **Flash repay** unwinds the account.
