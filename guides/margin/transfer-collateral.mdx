The **Transfer Collateral** tab moves a token in either direction:

| Direction | Label | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Wallet → margin | **MB** | Deposits into the margin account (creating it in the same transaction if needed) |
| Margin → wallet | **WB** | Withdraws to your wallet. Health-checked on-chain |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Transfer Collateral">
    On **Margin**, open the third tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose direction and token">
    Pick **MB** (into margin) or **WB** (to wallet), then the token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    The field is capped by the source balance and, for withdrawals, by the **safe limit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Check the preview and approve in your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The safe limit

With debt, you can withdraw only what keeps the account's health factor at **1.15 or above** (the app's safety floor). The program itself rejects anything that would leave the account at 1.10 or below.

With no debt, **100%** withdraws everything, down to the last unit.

## Limits on the wallet side

When you deposit SOL from the wallet, 0.05 SOL stays behind for transaction fees.
