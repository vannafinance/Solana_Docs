Portfolio is the home page. It combines your wallet, your Earn supply, your Predict positions and your margin account.

## Top cards

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Net worth** | Wallet + Lending + Margin equity (collateral minus debt), with a bar showing the split |
| **Net earnings** | Lending interest earned plus trading P&L, each shown as its own chip |
| **Health factor** | Your margin account's health factor with its zone (Safe / Warning / Danger), what you've borrowed, your leverage and the interest accrued so far. Shows **No debt** when you owe nothing |

## Lender tab

- **Lending pools**: Lender Stats (total holdings, number of supplied assets, average supply APY, interest earned), a 30-day Lending P&L chart and a positions table of every pool you supply.
- **Predict**: your prediction-market positions and what they earn while you wait.

## Trader tab

Your margin account in one place: collateral by asset, debt, health factor and leverage, plus each open Spot, Perps and Farm position. Every **Repay** button takes you straight to the Margin page's Repay tab with that asset selected.

## Where the numbers come from

All figures are read live from the fork: on-chain accounts priced with the same oracle prices the program uses (Kamino Scope first, Pyth as fallback). Nothing is cached in your browser, so clearing site data never changes what you see.
