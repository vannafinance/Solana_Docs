Portfolio is the combined view of wallet balances, Earn receipts, and margin-account collateral / debt.

## What you see

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| Credit LP | vToken balances and the underlying pool claim |
| Trader | Margin collateral, debt, health, and derived long/short / Farm legs |
| Spot | Wallet token balances used by Swap |

Numbers come from on-chain accounts plus Pyth prices. A zero P&L figure is not proof of realized performance if cost basis is still being reconstructed from history.

If a row looks empty, confirm you minted from Faucet and waited for the last transaction to confirm.
