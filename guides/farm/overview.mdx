Farm is **Leveraged Yield**. Deposit a stock or PreStock, borrow against it, and deploy into a pool that actually pays.

Kamino's **xStocks** market lists TSLAx and friends at ~0% supply APY. Kamino's **main market** pays a live USDC/SOL rate. Farm connects the two: deposit a stock as collateral, borrow USDC or SOL from Vanna at 1–5×, supply it to Kamino's main market. The stock stays as collateral, so you keep its price exposure.

## How it works

Deposit a stock as collateral, borrow USDC or SOL from Vanna at 1–5×, supply it to Kamino's main market. The stock stays as collateral. The Vanna debt is in the borrowed USDC or SOL and stays on the margin account.

The program also has a same-asset path (`lite_open`: borrow the same stock and supply it into a registered Kamino reserve). The app does not open positions through it; see the [Lite contract reference](/developers/contracts/lite).

Net APR ≈ Kamino supply APY − Vanna borrow APR. The screenshot's +0.67% at 2× is a live preview, not a guarantee.
