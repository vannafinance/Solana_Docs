**Analytics** is a live, public view of the whole protocol. It reads every margin account and pool directly from the chain. Nothing here sends a transaction.

<Frame caption="Analytics overview: bad debt, liquidatable accounts, accounts at risk and the accounts table. Click the image to zoom.">

The status bar at the top shows that the data is live, how many accounts were found, since when activity is counted, the last update time and a **Refresh** button.

## Sections

| Group | Page | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Risk** | **Overview** | Is anything wrong? Bad debt, liquidatable accounts and debt, accounts at risk, average health and leverage, total debt |
| | **Positions** | Look up any wallet or margin account; how accounts spread across health, leverage and collateral |
| | **Liquidations** | Past liquidations |
| | **Top accounts** | The largest accounts |
| | **Alerts** | Accounts and pools that need attention |
| **Markets** | **Lending Pools** | Supplied, borrowed, available, utilization and rates per pool |
| | **Collateral** | What collateral is made of |
| | **Prices** | Oracle prices in use |
| **Tools** | **What-if Calculator** | How a price move would change accounts' health |

## Overview

The cards answer the key questions at a glance:

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bad debt (estimated)** | Debt not covered by collateral. $0 means no account owes more than it holds |
| **Can be liquidated** | Accounts at a health factor of 1.10 or below, and their debt |
| **Liquidatable debt** | That debt, and the collateral backing it |
| **Accounts at risk / active** | Accounts close to the line, out of all active accounts |
| **Average health / leverage** | Across accounts with debt |
| **Collateral at risk** | Collateral in accounts with health under 1.50 |
| **Total debt** | Everything owed, against all collateral |

Below, the **Accounts** table lists every account **By health** or **By leverage**, filterable by Liquidatable, At risk, Safe, No debt and Not valued, with search by wallet or margin address. If you're connected, your own account is shown first.
